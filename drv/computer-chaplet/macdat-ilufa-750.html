Chaplet iLuFA 750

The Chaplet iLuFA 750 is a 486-based generic/ODM laptop that was released in early 1994.

This model was sold under Chaplet's own brand, as well as by Comtrade, who called it the Sound WinNote 486. In some markets, Chaplet sold this laptop under their older Halikan brand in some markets, so you will sometimes see it referenced as the Halikan 750.

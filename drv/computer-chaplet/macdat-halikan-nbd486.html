Chaplet Halikan NBD 486

The Chaplet Halikan NBD 486 Series are 486-based generic/ODM laptops that was released in 1993. They were sold by Chaplet themselves, as well as by other brands under their own names. There were three main models in the series - the NBD 486, NBD 486C, and NBD 486T. They differ by what display they use (Passive Matrix Grayscale, Passive Matrix Color, and Active Matrix Color respectively).

There were at least two distinct revisions of this laptop's motherboard, one with socketed CPUs and another with soldered CPUs. They both share the same model number, so they're not easy to tell apart from the outside. I believe the revision with soldered CPUs came first as it used an older video chip.
